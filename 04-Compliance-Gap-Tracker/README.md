**Scenario**

A growing financial services organization is preparing for an internal compliance review and anticipates future regulatory inspections. Over time, the organization has implemented multiple regulatory and governance requirements, but compliance activities are tracked inconsistently across departments, making it difficult to identify outstanding obligations, monitor remediation efforts, and provide consolidated compliance reporting to senior management.

To strengthen its Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) framework, the organization requires a centralized solution capable of mapping regulatory requirements to internal controls, assessing implementation status, identifying compliance gaps, prioritizing remediation activities, assigning accountability, and monitoring progress through executive dashboards.

**Project Overview**

Designed a structured Compliance Gap Assessment and Regulatory Action Tracker to support organizations in identifying, documenting, prioritizing, and monitoring compliance deficiencies against applicable legal, regulatory, and organizational requirements.

The workbook enables compliance professionals to perform systematic control assessments, evaluate implementation status, assign risk ratings, develop remediation plans, monitor progress, and provide executive reporting through automated dashboards.

**Expected Outcomes**

Centralized repository for tracking regulatory obligations, compliance gaps, and remediation activities.
Improved identification and prioritization of compliance deficiencies based on risk and business impact.
Enhanced accountability through clear assignment of action owners, deadlines, and implementation status.
Increased visibility into compliance performance using automated dashboards and key performance indicators (KPIs).

Strengthened governance by supporting proactive compliance monitoring and continuous improvement.
More informed management decision-making through real-time reporting on compliance status, risk exposure, and remediation effectiveness.
