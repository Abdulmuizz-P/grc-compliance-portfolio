**Project Scenario**

A healthcare technology company is launching a cloud-based digital health platform that enables patients to book medical appointments, access electronic health records, consult healthcare professionals remotely, receive electronic prescriptions, and make online healthcare payments. The platform processes large volumes of personal data and special category personal data, including patient identification information, medical histories, laboratory results, prescriptions, biometric authentication data, and payment information.

To support remote access and centralized healthcare delivery, patient information is stored on cloud infrastructure, with certain processing activities involving cross-border data transfers to third-party cloud service providers.

Given the nature, scope, context, and purposes of the processing, the organization must conduct a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) to identify and evaluate privacy risks, assess the necessity and proportionality of processing activities, implement appropriate technical and organizational safeguards, and demonstrate compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and the General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID) 2025 before the platform is deployed.

**Project Overview**

Developed a comprehensive, Excel-based Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) solution for a cloud-based digital health platform processing personal and sensitive health information. The workbook provides a structured methodology for documenting processing activities, identifying privacy risks,

evaluating existing controls, assessing cross-border data transfers, implementing mitigation measures, and supporting privacy-by-design throughout the project lifecycle.

The solution incorporates automated risk scoring, control assessment, residual risk evaluation, mitigation planning, and executive reporting to support informed decision-making, regulatory compliance, and organizational accountability.
